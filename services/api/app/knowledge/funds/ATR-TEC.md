# Atrium Technology (ATR-TEC)

## Objective

Concentrated growth from the US technology sector.

## What it holds

Atrium Technology holds 2 exchange-traded funds in fixed proportions, rebalanced monthly. Its largest underlying companies are NVIDIA Corp (16.4%), Apple Inc (14.5%), Microsoft Corp (11.0%), Broadcom Inc (4.6%), Micron Technology Inc (4.4%).

## Who it suits

Thematic conviction as a small satellite allocation; high loss tolerance.

It is risk band 5 of 5 (Adventurous). It suits investors whose risk profile is band 5 or higher.

## Fees

Atrium fee: 0.50% a year, which is $50 on every $10,000 invested.

Underlying ETF costs: about 0.08% a year, from each ETF's published expense ratio. They are already reflected in the ETF prices.

Total yearly cost: about 0.58%, or $58 on $10,000. There are no entry, exit, switching or performance fees.

## How it behaved

From July 2007 to September 2026, the fund's mix returned 16.9% a year on average before the Atrium fee, with yearly swings (volatility) of about 19.8%.

Its best calendar year was 2009 (+55.5%) and its worst was 2008 (-42.0%).

Its largest fall from a peak was 49.9%, from October 2007 to February 2009.

## Risks

This fund holds a single sector, so it can fall far more than the overall market and stay down for years.

Hold it only as a small part of a wider portfolio.

## Important information

The fund launched on October 1, 2024. Figures before then are a backtest of its target mix using the ETFs' real month-end prices. The backtest starts in July 2007.

Backtests are hypothetical. Past performance does not predict future returns, and you can lose money. Atrium is a prototype with synthetic accounts and paper trading; nothing here is investment advice.
