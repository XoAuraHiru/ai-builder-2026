# Atrium Growth (ATR-GRW)

## Objective

Long-term capital growth from large-cap and growth-oriented US equities.

## What it holds

Atrium Growth holds 3 exchange-traded funds in fixed proportions, rebalanced monthly. Its largest underlying companies are NVIDIA Corp (9.3%), Apple Inc (8.2%), Microsoft Corp (6.6%), Amazon.com Inc (4.1%), Alphabet Inc Class A (3.5%).

| ETF | Weight | Asset class | Region | ETF expense ratio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VOO | 50% | Equity | US | 0.03% |
| QQQ | 30% | Equity | US | 0.18% |
| VUG | 20% | Equity | US | 0.03% |

## Who it suits

Long horizons over seven years and higher loss tolerance.

It is risk band 4 of 5 (Growth). It suits investors whose risk profile is band 4 or higher.

## Fees

Atrium fee: 0.40% a year, which is $40 on every $10,000 invested.

Underlying ETF costs: about 0.07% a year, from each ETF's published expense ratio. They are already reflected in the ETF prices.

Total yearly cost: about 0.47%, or $47 on $10,000. There are no entry, exit, switching or performance fees.

## How it behaved

From July 2007 to September 2026, the fund's mix returned 13.5% a year on average before the Atrium fee, with yearly swings (volatility) of about 16.9%.

Its best calendar year was 2009 (+47.0%) and its worst was 2008 (-40.2%).

Its largest fall from a peak was 48.6%, from October 2007 to February 2009.

## Risks

This fund is concentrated in large US growth and technology companies, so it can fall much further than the overall market.

Falls of a third or more have happened in past crashes, and recoveries have taken years.

## Important information

The fund launched on October 1, 2024. Figures before then are a backtest of its target mix using the ETFs' real month-end prices. The backtest starts in July 2007. VOO has prices from September 2010; before that, its 50% weight is spread across the fund's other ETFs.

Backtests are hypothetical. Past performance does not predict future returns, and you can lose money. Atrium is a prototype with synthetic accounts and paper trading; nothing here is investment advice.
