# Atrium Balanced (ATR-BAL)

## Objective

Balanced growth and income through a 60/40 mix of US equities and bonds.

## What it holds

Atrium Balanced holds 2 exchange-traded funds in fixed proportions, rebalanced monthly. Its largest underlying companies are NVIDIA Corp (4.1%), Apple Inc (3.8%), Microsoft Corp (3.1%), Amazon.com Inc (2.0%), Alphabet Inc Class A (1.6%).

| ETF | Weight | Asset class | Region | ETF expense ratio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VTI | 60% | Equity | US | 0.03% |
| BND | 40% | Bond | US | 0.03% |

## Who it suits

Medium horizons of three to seven years and moderate loss tolerance.

It is risk band 3 of 5 (Balanced). It suits investors whose risk profile is band 3 or higher.

## Fees

Atrium fee: 0.35% a year, which is $35 on every $10,000 invested.

Underlying ETF costs: about 0.03% a year, from each ETF's published expense ratio. They are already reflected in the ETF prices.

Total yearly cost: about 0.38%, or $38 on $10,000. There are no entry, exit, switching or performance fees.

## How it behaved

From July 2007 to September 2026, the fund's mix returned 7.8% a year on average before the Atrium fee, with yearly swings (volatility) of about 10.2%.

Its best calendar year was 2019 (+21.8%) and its worst was 2008 (-21.5%).

Its largest fall from a peak was 32.2%, from October 2007 to February 2009.

## Risks

With 60% in US stocks, this fund can fall sharply in a market crash, as it did in 2008.

Bonds usually soften stock falls, but not always: in 2022 stocks and bonds fell together.

## Important information

The fund launched on October 1, 2024. Figures before then are a backtest of its target mix using the ETFs' real month-end prices. The backtest starts in July 2007.

Backtests are hypothetical. Past performance does not predict future returns, and you can lose money. Atrium is a prototype with synthetic accounts and paper trading; nothing here is investment advice.
