Why Atrium

How Atrium invests

Atrium offers seven house funds. Each one is a fixed mix of low-cost exchange-traded funds (ETFs) listed in the US, rebalanced monthly, ranging from a Treasury-bill reserve (risk band 1) to a technology fund (risk band 5).

Every investor gets a risk profile with a band from 1 to 5, and Atrium checks each instruction against it before accepting it.

Clear fees

Each fund has one yearly Atrium fee, between 0.10% and 0.50%, plus the costs of the underlying ETFs. There are no entry, exit, switching or performance fees.

Everything explained

Every score, projection and recommendation in Atrium shows why: the evidence, the method and, where AI wrote the words, which model wrote them.

Projections come from a simulation engine run over real market history since 2007, not from the AI.

How Atrium protects you

Atrium checks identity and fraud signals from the first contact, before an account is opened.

Instructions that would take you above your risk profile need your explicit confirmation.

Important information

Atrium is a prototype built for the AI Builder Cup 2026. Its people and accounts are synthetic and all trading is on paper. Nothing here is investment advice.
