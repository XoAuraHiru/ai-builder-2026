Atrium Global Equity (ATR-GLB)

Objective

Diversified growth from developed and emerging markets outside and including the US.

What it holds

Atrium Global Equity holds 3 exchange-traded funds in fixed proportions, rebalanced monthly. Its largest underlying companies are NVIDIA Corp (2.2%), Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co Ltd (2.0%), Apple Inc (1.9%), Microsoft Corp (1.6%), Samsung Electronics Co Ltd (1.1%).

Who it suits

Investors who want exposure beyond the US with long horizons.

It is risk band 4 of 5 (Growth). It suits investors whose risk profile is band 4 or higher.

Fees

Atrium fee: 0.40% a year, which is $40 on every $10,000 invested.

Underlying ETF costs: about 0.05% a year, from each ETF's published expense ratio. They are already reflected in the ETF prices.

Total yearly cost: about 0.45%, or $45 on $10,000. There are no entry, exit, switching or performance fees.

How it behaved

From August 2007 to September 2026, the fund's mix returned 6.4% a year on average before the Atrium fee, with yearly swings (volatility) of about 16.8%.

Its best calendar year was 2009 (+31.2%) and its worst was 2008 (-40.1%).

Its largest fall from a peak was 55.4%, from October 2007 to February 2009.

Risks

Foreign stocks add currency risk: a stronger dollar lowers the value of overseas holdings.

International markets have trailed the US for long stretches, and emerging markets can be volatile.

Important information

The fund launched on October 1, 2024. Figures before then are a backtest of its target mix using the ETFs' real month-end prices. The backtest starts in August 2007. VT has prices from June 2008; before that, its 50% weight is spread across the fund's other ETFs. VXUS has prices from January 2011; before that, its 30% weight is spread across the fund's other ETFs.

Backtests are hypothetical. Past performance does not predict future returns, and you can lose money. Atrium is a prototype with synthetic accounts and paper trading; nothing here is investment advice.
