Foundations for the Atrium revamp

A white canvas, white cards outlined with a sand hairline, and one forest green for action and money. Engine figures use a grotesk with tabular numerals; anything Gemini writes uses a serif, so provenance is visible at a glance.

01

Engines count, Gemini explains

Numbers from the pricing, simulation and matching engines are set in Schibsted Grotesk. Gemini’s words are set in Newsreader, always with model and cost.

02

People decide

Every AI proposal ends in a button a person presses. Two-person actions show who asked, who approves and the code step.

03

Quiet until it matters

Sand and green carry every page. Coral and amber are kept for risk, holds and alerts, so they stand out when they appear.
